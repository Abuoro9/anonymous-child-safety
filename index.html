Official Safety Policy

Child Safety Standards

Anonymous is committed to maintaining a safe environment and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Anonymous and its developer, Nexusconsole, maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), and any activity that endangers minors.

1. About These Standards

These Child Safety Standards apply to Anonymous, an application developed and published by Nexusconsole.

These standards establish our commitment to preventing, identifying, addressing, and reporting child sexual abuse and exploitation associated with the use of our application.

These standards apply to all users and all interactions taking place through Anonymous.

2. Strict Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM

Anonymous strictly prohibits all forms of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Creating, sharing, requesting, distributing, possessing, or promoting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of anyone under the age of 18.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or manipulation of minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual extortion, coercion, or threats involving minors.
  • Sharing sexualized images, videos, or other exploitative material involving children.
  • Attempting to contact or manipulate minors for sexual exploitation.
  • Encouraging, facilitating, or promoting any form of child sexual abuse.

Any user found engaging in these activities may have their access to Anonymous permanently restricted or terminated.

3. Child Safety and Age Restrictions

Anonymous is not intended for children. We are committed to protecting minors from inappropriate interactions and exploitation.

We do not permit users to use Anonymous to engage in sexual, exploitative, or otherwise harmful interactions involving minors.

We will take appropriate action when we become aware of activity that violates these standards, including restricting access where necessary.

4. Reporting Violations

Users who encounter suspected child exploitation, abusive behavior, grooming, or CSAM associated with Anonymous should report it immediately.

Reports can be submitted directly to our designated Child Safety Contact using the contact information provided below.

Please include relevant information that can help us investigate the report, such as:

  • A description of the suspected violation.
  • Relevant usernames or room identifiers, if available.
  • The approximate date and time of the incident.
  • Any relevant information that can help identify the reported activity.

Do not download, forward, or redistribute suspected CSAM when making a report.

We will review submitted reports and take appropriate action based on the available information.

5. Handling and Removal of CSAM

Nexusconsole is committed to responding promptly to credible reports involving CSAM and child sexual exploitation.

Our response procedures include:

  1. Reviewing reports involving suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  2. Taking appropriate action to restrict or terminate accounts involved in confirmed violations.
  3. Removing or restricting access to prohibited material where technically possible.
  4. Preserving relevant information when legally permitted and necessary for an investigation.
  5. Escalating confirmed CSAM cases to the appropriate authorities or designated child protection organizations, as required by applicable laws.

We will cooperate with lawful investigations concerning child safety and exploitation.

6. User Feedback and Safety Concerns

We encourage users to report safety concerns, suspicious activity, and violations of these standards.

Users can contact our Child Safety Contact directly to submit concerns or provide information about potentially harmful activity.

Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately. We do not tolerate retaliation against individuals who submit genuine child safety reports.

7. Compliance with Applicable Laws

Nexusconsole is committed to complying with applicable child protection, online safety, and child sexual exploitation laws and regulations.

We will cooperate with relevant law enforcement agencies and child protection authorities where legally required and appropriate.

8. Encryption and Child Safety

Anonymous uses encrypted communications to protect the privacy of its users.

Privacy and encryption do not authorize or permit the use of Anonymous for child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or the distribution of CSAM.

Where a safety concern is reported, we will assess the available information and take appropriate action consistent with our technical capabilities and applicable legal obligations.
